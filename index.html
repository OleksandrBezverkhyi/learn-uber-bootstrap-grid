8 reasons to start working with us

No cash, payment is made automatically

The cost of the trip is paid by the passenger from a linked bank card, i.e. there are no risks associated with carrying cash for either the passenger or the driver, and you don't need to look for small change.

High earnings with guaranteed payouts

We deposit your earnings directly to your personal bank account. No need to register a company, pay business taxes, file returns, or deal with accounting and bank account management.

Comfortable, transparent, and simple connection

We connect you remotely and free of charge, so you don't need to come to us! You send us copies of your documents, we register you and send you the connection details, and in 1-2 days you can start working!

24/7 support

Uber provides round-the-clock support for drivers via email. If you have any questions, you can always ask them through the online consultation service.

Commission 8% + BONUSES

We have minimal commissions and pay bonuses for every trip, while some of our competitors keep the bonuses for themselves. We work honestly, and drivers earn more with us!

Work at a convenient time, no schedules

You simply press the button to log in to start working, and when you decide to finish, you press the button to log out. You can work only in the morning, in the evening, on weekends, or on holidays!

Always a large number of orders

The app shows where there are currently the most orders and where higher rates apply. You won't have any downtime or empty runs. Our passengers are always pleasant and completely non-confrontational people who are a pleasure to drive.

No taxi signs or decals

You don’t need to repaint your car, add taxi lights, or cover it with stickers to drive with Uber.
